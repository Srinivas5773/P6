❓ How to Play Memory Match

Goal: Flip cards to reveal hidden SVG icons and match identical pairs to clear the board!


Controls:

  • Mouse / Touch: Click or tap any card to flip it over.
  • Keyboard: Use Arrow Keys to move focus across the card grid, and press Enter or Spacebar to flip.

Game Modes:

  • Classic: Standard 2-card matching.
  • Triple: Challenge yourself by matching 3 identical cards per set!
  • Time Attack: Race against a ticking countdown clock.
  • Quest: Complete progressive campaign levels with star targets.
  • Vs AI: Compete against an adaptive AI opponent.
